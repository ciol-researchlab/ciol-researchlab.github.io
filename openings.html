Openings and Collaborations

CIOL is always looking for strong, self-motivated, and hardworking research students and collaborators. If you are interested in joining or collaborating with the lab, please email your CV and a short personal description, including your research interests, to ciol.management@gmail.com.

Students and researchers from any university are welcome to join us, provided that their research interests align with the lab's areas of work. We are open to everyone who is enthusiastic about learning, contributing, and collaborating.

Current availability

CIOL is not currently starting any new projects. We plan to begin new projects again in January 2027, so if you are looking to join an upcoming project, please wait until then. Or, you can email and We'll reach out to you when we have new projects available. We will also post updates on our website and social media channels when new projects are available.

However, if you have your own research idea and would like guidance from CIOL, you may contact us now. Please include "" | OWN IDEA" in your email subject line and provide a short overview of what you would like to work on. This will help us compare your idea with our relevant experience and understand your aims, such as preparing a publication or pursuing higher studies, so that we can check whether a suitable supervisor is available to support you.

We look forward to hearing from you at ciol.management@gmail.com.
