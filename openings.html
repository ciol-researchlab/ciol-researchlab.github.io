Openings and Collaborations

CIOL is always looking for strong, self-motivated, and hardworking research students and collaborators. If you are interested in joining or collaborating with the lab, please email your CV and a short personal description, including your research interests, to ciol.management@gmail.com.

Students and researchers from any university are welcome to join us, provided that their research interests align with the lab's areas of work. We are open to everyone who is enthusiastic about learning, contributing, and collaborating.

Current availability

Joining a new CIOL project

CIOL is not currently starting any new projects. We plan to begin new projects again in January 2027. If you are interested in joining an upcoming project, please wait until then. You may also email us, and we will reach out when new projects become available. Updates will also be posted on our website and social media channels.

Only a few people, around 2 to 5, will be accepted into these new projects, as preference is given to existing lab members. If you are particularly interested in joining CIOL and working with us, we suggest bringing your own research idea, as described below.

Working on your own research idea

If you have your own research idea and would like guidance from CIOL, you may contact us now. Please include "OWN IDEA" in your email subject line and provide a short overview of what you would like to work on and your aim.

This will help us compare your idea with our relevant experience and understand your goals, such as preparing a publication or pursuing higher studies. We can then check whether a suitable supervisor is available to support you.

We look forward to hearing from you at ciol.management@gmail.com.
